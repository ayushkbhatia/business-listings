## 2 · Why the tracker cannot sequence this on its own

**The Export Tracker records design exports. It does not record what is built in code.** Those two
have drifted, and we only found out by asking.

`12c`'s second pass checked the 8 Sep handoff against the working tree at `00ed09a`. Of the three
boards that handoff proposed as next work:

- **`11e` sponsored placement** — listed as blocked. Already shipped: page, `SlotList`,
  `lib/placement/service.ts`, the `PlacementSlot` model, the nav entry, `placeSponsored()`.
- **`4h` reports, flags & disputes** — listed as never exported and owed `11c`'s dispute queue.
  Already built at `app/(admin)/admin/reports`, queue included.
- **`3m`'s cancellation banner** — listed as a needed re-export. Already shipped.

Two of three were done. On the board itself, **six of eight build notes were already delivered**, and
the render omitted a control that was live on the screen.

That is what happens when front end and back end are refined per screen in parallel while the design
record only advances on export. So **Phase 0 is a reconciliation pass**, and every phase below states
its dependency claims as claims. If a board turns out to be built, its work changes from *design a
screen* to *export against the tree and correct the drift* — about a third of the effort and a
different kind of output.

**This applies doubly to the S lane.** Twenty-eight boards were drawn in six days against an audit of
the tree, not against the tree itself. Expect some to be partly built already.

## 4 · The amendment `12c` owes

`12c` has shipped twice and mentions none of this. **Spec completeness is 12 of the 100 ranking
points, and services are held out of the spec-template system entirely** — so a service supplier can
never earn those 12. It is the same structural zero as `coverage` and `stock` in the fan-out, in a
different file, and it is worse on browse pages: under `redistribute`, relevance's points spread
across the remaining five in proportion, so the share a service supplier is locked out of *grows*.

**Scope completeness is the parallel measure** and it must land before the services vector publishes,
or half the directory ranks 12 points light. The fix is a second weight vector on the same board —
`RankingWeights` gains a kind key, the editor gains a toggle, and the draft, preview, publish,
history and disclosure are all shared. That is `12c-s`, Phase 6.

Also for the amendment: the effective-vector plan ceiling (`B9`) applies to both vectors, and the
affected-seller count must be **scoped to the vector**, or a services publish claims to have told
30,000 parts suppliers.

## 5 · The phases

Eleven phases, 72 boards, each placed once. The ordering principle is unchanged: **the admin console
owns the models the other surfaces render, so a config board precedes the boards that display its
output.** The service track adds a second: **a `-s` variant follows the decision that shapes it, not
the base board it resembles.**

## 9 · The shape of it

**Phase 0 is not overhead.** It is the phase that stops the other ten being designed twice. The
evidence is on the board we re-exported: eight build notes, six already shipped, one control missing
from the render, two of three proposed follow-ons already built.

After that it is two ideas repeated. **Whoever owns the model goes before whoever renders it** —
`4d`, `4i`, `12e`, `4e-s` own models; `12a`→`4c` is a pipeline; `10g` renders it; `4a` renders
everything. And **the fork goes before everything it forks** — `4d-s` first, then the seller's slice
end to end, then the rest of the track in the order the decisions were taken.
